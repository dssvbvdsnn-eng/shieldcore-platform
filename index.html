QUESTIONS / ANSWERS

Important questions.
Clear answers.

A quick overview of the platform concept and its proposed model.

Does ShieldCore store customer data?+

The proposed design uses Zero-Knowledge Proofs to reduce the need to expose or retain personal data. Implementation details and retention policies must be defined and tested before launch.

Does it replace an existing payment gateway?+

No. The concept adds an API inspection layer alongside existing systems. Integrations can be designed for the payment providers and platforms already used by each customer.

Who are the target customers?+

Start-up e-commerce businesses, emerging financial services apps, and software agencies building solutions for their clients.

Are the displayed figures actual results?+

No. The displayed figures are targets and proposed indicators from the project report. They are not published results from operational testing or a security certification.
